PO and Invoice can have different tax rates. These are applied separately in all cost calculations and Excel exports.

📖 Date Types — How They Compare

📅 Planned Dates
The live, working schedule. Updates whenever you edit, drag a bar, or the system reschedules from a predecessor.
🔒 Baseline Dates
A frozen snapshot of the plan taken when you locked baseline. The contract reference — never moves on its own.
✅ Actual Dates
What really happened. Set when activity goes In Progress / Complete (or auto-filled from labor PO ≥90%).
